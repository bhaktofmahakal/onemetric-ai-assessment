**01 MARKETING & GTM** 

**Dynamic Campaign Segmentation** 

Assessing problem framing, decision design and automation-forward thinking 

**BRIEF** 

A client runs multiple campaigns across different business units. A prospect is currently enrolled in a campaign centred on Product B. New third-party intent data now indicates meaningful interest in Product A. Similar changes may occur repeatedly as new behavioural, firmographic and intent signals arrive. Continuing the original journey may create irrelevant communication, while switching too quickly may cause conflicting messages or overreaction to a weak signal. 

**YOUR CHALLENGE** 

**Design an automation or AI-agent-led approach that keeps segmentation current and determines whether the prospect should continue, pause, exit or move to another campaign journey.** 

**EXPECTED** 

● Explain how you would distinguish a meaningful change in interest from a temporary or low-confidence signal. 

● Define the decision logic for continuing, pausing, switching or escalating the journey. ● Address conflicting product signals, communication frequency and cross-business-unit ownership. ● Show where automation can act independently and where marketing or sales should review the decision. ● Describe how the system should learn from downstream engagement and conversion outcomes. 

**INTEGRATION** 

● Identify the data sources, systems and objects the solution would need. 

● Explain the trigger and the sequence of reads, decisions and write-backs. 

● Describe how campaign tools, CRM/CDP records and third-party intent data remain synchronised. ● Include safeguards for duplicate enrolment, stale data, missing identifiers and failed actions. 

You may use diagrams, assumptions or pseudocode where they help explain your approach. Name specific tools only when the choice materially affects the solution.
